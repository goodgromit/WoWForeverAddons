# The stories from Spoken

The whole addon is all rights reserved (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), except for what this file covers:
the dungeon and boss stories that come from Spoken.

Wherever Spoken tells the story of one of the journal's dungeons or bosses, the journal shows
Spoken's own text, word for word, so that it matches what Spoken reads aloud. It is used with the
permission of Spoken's author (2026-11-14). The text is in `Data/Generated/SpokenLore.lua`, in English
and in each of the journal's other languages that Spoken has it in. Where the player has Spoken
installed, the journal reads the same stories from it.

Spoken adapted most of these stories from warcraft.wiki.gg. The wiki's text is licensed under the Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

As adapted from the wiki, the story text is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 too, with credit to the
contributors of warcraft.wiki.gg. Spoken rewrote the wiki's text in its own voice and translated it;
the journal copies Spoken's text unchanged. Each dungeon story adapted from the wiki names its page
in `Data/Generated/SpokenLore.lua`. Spoken wrote its boss stories from the game's Adventure Guide and
from the wiki. The Adventure Guide's text, like the rest of the game, is Blizzard Entertainment's.

A few dungeon stories have no wiki page as their source, as `Data/Generated/SpokenLore.lua` notes
beside each one: Spoken wrote those itself (The Hall of Thanes, Excavation Site: Wetlands, Razorfen
Kraul, Krol'dok Stronghold, Blackmaw Hold and the Shaper's Terrace). They are used with its author's
permission.

Only this story text is covered here. The rest of the addon (its code, its other data and text, its
maps and pictures) stays all rights reserved: see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
